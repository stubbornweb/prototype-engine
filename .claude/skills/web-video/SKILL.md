# Web Video

Use video when passive cinematic movement is more efficient or visually appropriate than realtime rendering.

Provide:
- poster image
- muted autoplay/playsinline behavior when appropriate
- mobile fallback
- reduced-motion/static treatment
- compressed web delivery

Video is progressive enhancement: the page must be complete and persuasive on the poster alone, and the video must never delay the H1 or CTA. Skip it under `prefers-reduced-motion` and `Save-Data`. Prefer footage that shows the real product, people, or result over abstract atmosphere. Stay inside the media budget in `docs/customer-first.md` §4.

Keep the subject/composition stable enough for typography to remain readable.
